My dearest Annabell,

I wanted to make something special and unique using the skills I’ve learned in this semester. I hope you love it. I know that I did something that hurt you, and I regret it so much I will never do anything to lose you, I promise. I know saying "I'm sorry" cannot simply erase how you feel. I don't want to ignore your feelings or make you feel like it's what ever to me. I want you to know that I understand that my actions hurt you, and I genuinely regret causing you that pain.

You are someone who means so much to me, and not just to me to so many more people you are just the sweetes most loving girl. Thank you for being my person, the perfect girl, and my princess.

I love you, Annabell. Now Then and Forever. ♡

— Ulises
